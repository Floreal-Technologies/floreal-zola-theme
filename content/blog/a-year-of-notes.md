A year ago we started writing one short note a week instead of one long post a quarter.

<!-- more -->

## Why short

A long post waits for a free afternoon that rarely comes. A short note fits
in the gap after a build finishes. Fifty short notes taught us more than
four long ones would have.

## What stuck

The notes that held up a year later were the ones about one thing that
went wrong, and the one line that fixed it.
