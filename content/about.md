This is the demo site of the Floréal theme. It stands here so that a change to
a template can be seen without a second repository.

A page like this one is a page that stands on its own: it belongs to no
section, it carries no date, and it is reached from the header bar. Write
`content/about.md`, add its entry to `[[extra.nav]]`, and the theme draws it
with `page.html`, the template Zola reaches for when nothing names another.
